Settle your decisions first, build toward launch on six tracks at once, make the threat model the last gate, and start AI once real users exist.

If you need to cut or reorder

  • Launch sooner: trim visual identity to #228, #227, #229, #250, #369, #230, #231 and #232, and move the #233 screen redesign after launch. That track is the largest in stage 3.
  • The hard minimum: the ten launch blockers in #199 and the AWS work they need. Visible bugs and the P3 security items are my judgment, and they can slip if you accept the risk.
  • AI earlier: the platform can start once #202 exists, because evals use synthetic data. Launch would slip by about 35 label-days. I would only do this if showing AI work matters more to you than having users.

This order is my recommendation, based on the priority labels, the epic plans and about 30 issue bodies, most read in part. It moves #369 and #263 ahead of their labels and #271's own step order.
